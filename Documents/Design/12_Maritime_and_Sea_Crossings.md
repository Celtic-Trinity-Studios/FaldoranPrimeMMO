# 12 Maritime, Sea Crossings & Exploration

In the planetary-scale world of Faldoran Prime, the sea is the most significant economic artery and the most dangerous frontier. This document outlines the comprehensive design for maritime travel, construction, and global exploration.

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy of the Sea
Sea crossings are not "cinematic transitions"—they are **high-stakes logistical projects**. 
- **Bulk Economy:** Portals are for people and high-value artifacts. **Ships move Civilization.** Bulk resources (Iron, Timber, Siderite) are too heavy for portals and must be transported via sea lanes.
- **Scale Intensity:** Crossing a major ocean is a **multi-day (real-time)** commitment. The ship becomes a player's home, workshop, and fortress during the voyage.

---

## 2. Ship Construction: The "Mega-Template"
Ships are built using the [10 Dynamic Crafting](file:///e:/FaldoranPrimeMMO/Documents/Design/10_Dynamic_Crafting_and_Templates.md) system. Unlike a sword, a ship is a collection of "Sub-Templates" socketed into a **Vessel Frame**.

### Material Composition
The materials used in construction dictate the ship's survivability in different "Sea Tiers":
- **Base Wood (Oak/Cedar):** Standard coastal sailing. Susceptible to "Abyssal Pressure" damage in deep water.
- **Metal Plating (Iron/Steel):** Increases Hull Integrity but adds weight, slowing the vessel. High resistance to sea monster physical damage.
- **Arcane Infusions:** 
    - **Sylph-Silk (Sails):** High-tier fabric infused with portal dust; catches magical currents.
    - **Etheric Varnish (Hull):** Reduces drag; essential for high-speed "Clipper" builds.
    - **Abyssal Plates:** Infusing metal with minerals found only in irradiated zones; allows a ship to traverse the "Abyssal Deep" without the hull imploding.

### The Crystal Core
Every ship above Tier 1 requires a **Crystal Core**—a central housing for Mana Crystals that powers the ship's automation, engines, and magical defenses.

## 4. Arcane Propulsion & Speed Mechanics
Magic serves as the "Nitro" for maritime travel, pushing ships beyond medieval limits.

- **The "Wind-Weaver" Effect:** Magic-infused sails treat "Headwinds" as neutral and "Tailwinds" as a 2x bonus.
- **Mana Engines (The Steamer Tier):** A hybrid engine that burns Mana Crystals to provide a flat +10 to +20 kn boost regardless of wind. These are extremely volatile; if the engine overheats, it can destroy the ship's deck.
- **Scale Dilation (Deep Water):** Once out of sight of land, the world enters a 1:10 coordinate scale.
    - **Example:** A 14 kn Xebec covers 140 "World Miles" per hour in Deep Water.
    - **Duration:** A 3000-mile crossing takes **~21 hours** (Real-Time).

## 5. Exploration & "Free Sailing"
Venturing toward the unknown requires a balance of speed and vigilance.

### The Scouting Hierarchy
- **Cruising (1:10 Scale):** Maximum travel efficiency. Tiny islands will not render or be detected. Best for known trade routes.
- **Active Scouting (1:5 Scale):** Efficiency is halved, but the ship's "Spotting Radius" is doubled. This is how players discover **Procedural Cayes** and **Resource Islands**.
- **The Coastal Snap (1:1 Scale):** Within 10 miles of land, the game snaps to 1:1 scale. Ship combat, reef navigation, and landing always happen at 1:1.

### Spotting & Gear
Discovery is driven by **Observation Affinity** and gear quality:
- **Crow's Nest:** Determines the base detection sphere.
- **Infused Spyglasses:** Can "Scan" landmasses from a distance to reveal resource signatures (e.g., "High-Yield Coal Detected") without landing.

## 6. Logic & Automation (Marine Scripts)
Ships are the primary use cases for [09 Logic Gems](file:///e:/FaldoranPrimeMMO/Documents/Design/09_Logic_and_Automation.md).

- **Navigation Gems:** Allow a ship to follow a "Lighthouse Trail" autonomously while the crew is offline.
- **Auto-Trim:** Logic that micro-manages sail angles 100x faster than human crews, providing a 10% speed bonus.
- **Anti-Leviathan Scripts:** Automated weapon systems (Ballistae, Harpoons) that trigger when a specific NPC Signature enters range.

---

## 7. Life on the Long Haul (The Voyage)
Because voyages take real-world hours/days, the ship must be self-sustaining.

- **The Mess Hall:** Crew must stock food/water. If stores run out, players accrue **Soul Debt** and "Rusty" penalties due to starvation.
- **Hull Maintenance:** Pasive wear from salt and storms requires a "Carpenter" to perform repairs using resources from the hold.
- **Social Leveling:** Ships are the best place for "Downtime Skills"—minigames, alchemy, or refining materials while in transit.

---

## 8. Sinking, Wrecks & Salvage
- **The Sinking State:** If durability hits 0, the ship becomes a **Temporary POI**.
- **Shipwreck POI:** The bulk cargo stays at the bottom of the ocean.
- **Salvage:** Recovery requires specialized "Diving Bells" or high-tier Water Magic. Salvaging a rival guild's sunken galleon can be more profitable than the original trade run.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Interdependence:** A captain provides the ship, the navigator provides the gems, and the crew provides the labor. No single player can easily conquer the "Great Blue" alone.
