# 02 World and Scale

Faldoran Prime features a **Planetary Scale (Earth-Sized) World** (~510 million km²), presenting a true frontier for exploration and civilization building.

## The Reality of Scale
- **Earth Scale Land Area:** ~148 million km².
- **Traversal:** Circumnavigation on foot could take 10+ years; on a fast mount, 1-2 years.
- **Development Strategy:** Handcrafting is impossible. The world uses a **Deterministic Seeded Procedure** (Procedural Layer) overlaid with handcrafted POIs and player-built Guild Towns.

## Traversal & Connectivity
In a world this large, **Fast Travel is a high-cost infrastructure project and a geopolitical resource.**

### Guild-Built Portals
- **Construction:** Requires rare "Portal Shards" and massive resource sinks (Stone, Timber, Metals).
- **The Portal Heart:** Portals are physical structures anchored to the planetary grid.
- **Maintenance:** Portals require constant mineral supplies and NPC "Portal Wardens" who must be fed crops/food.
- **Diplomacy:** Guilds own portals, set access fees (in resources or guild currency), and receive 100% of revenue.

### Portal Failure States
If maintenance requirements (Warden food or Mineral supplies) are not met, the portal degrades:
1.  **State: Starved (No Food):** NPCs abandon posts. The portal is inactive.
2.  **State: Depleted (No Minerals):** The portal becomes "Unstable." Travel has a 50% chance to fail, consuming resources but not teleporting.
3.  **State: Wild (Critical Neglect):** The portal connects to a random "Wilderness" exit coordinate on the same continent instead of its intended destination.

### Mounts and Caravans
- Mounts are a core mechanic for mid-range traversal.
- Caravan systems facilitate the "Silk Road" dynamics required for regional trade.

## Mechanical Implications
- **Distance = Difficulty:** Rare items in one region are common in another.
- **Ghost Town Risk:** Addressed by starting players in "Starter Continents" or hubs before expanding into the deep wilderness.
- **Migration Cycles:** As resources deplete, entire alliances may need to coordinate days-long real-time migrations to new continents.

### Absolute Scarcity (Non-Renewables)
The planet's crust is finite. This drives the geopolitical engine.
- **Metals & Minerals:** Iron, Coal, and Gold veins **do not respawn**. Once a mountain is stripped, it is empty forever.
- **Trade Towns:** Depletion turns mining rigs into "Trade Hubs" or eventually "Ghost Towns," forcing guilds to secure new territory or trade for essentials.
- **Flora & Fauna:** Trees and wildlife use a "Biological Tick" system. They regrow/reproduce slowly if not over-harvested. Over-harvesting leads to local extinction.
