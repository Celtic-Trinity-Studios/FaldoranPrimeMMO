# 07 Death and Consequences

Death in Faldoran Prime is a significant event that ripples through the social and economic fabric of the world. It is designed to discourage reckless play without forcing a total character reset, emphasizing the "Civilization Building" pillar.

## 1. The Soul Debt (XP & Skill Impact)
Upon death, a player incurs **Soul Debt**.
- **The "Rusty" Kickstart:** Death immediately applies a flat amount of XP Debt to all active skills, pushing them closer to the "Rusty" state.
- **Recovery:** This debt is cleared through normal gameplay (experience gain). It does not lower your Rank Permanence, only your current effective efficiency.

## 2. Equipment & Inventory
Consequences vary based on the **Security Tier** of the region where death occurred.

| Region Type | Equipped Item Penalty | Inventory Penalty | Lootability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sanctuary** | 5% Durability Loss | 10% Random Drop | Private Corpse |
| **Settled** | 10% Durability Loss | 20% Random Drop | Private (30m) -> Public |
| **Wilderness** | 20% Durability Loss | 250% Random Drop | Public (Immediate) |
| **Irradiated/Dungeons** | 30% Durability Loss | 50% Drop | Public (Immediate) |

- **Equipped Gear Protection:** Equipped items are **never** dropped on death. Instead, they take a significant "Durability Hit" (see [06 Gear and Combat](file:///e:/FaldoranPrimeMMO/Documents/Design/06_Gear_and_Combat.md) for Durability vs. Damage mechanics).
- **Random Drop Logic:** The "Inventory Penalty" refers to the percentage of non-equipped stacks that are dropped. Rare items should have a weighted "Weight" to influence their drop chance.

## 3. The "Civilization" Loop: Resurrection & Spawning
Spawning is tied to infrastructure, not just a menu button.

### Bind Points
- **Nature Bind (Solo):** Spawning at a random "Primal Shrine." High risk, no gear recovery.
- **Civ-Bind (Group):** Spawning at a Guild Town "Resurrection Obelisk."
    - **Requirement:** Obelisk must be powered by Mana Crystals and maintained by NPCs.
    - **Benefit:** Reduces Soul Debt and provides a temporary "Vigor" buff.
