# 10 Dynamic Crafting and Templates

Faldoran Prime rejects static "Recipe Books." Instead, crafting is a high-stakes experimentation system where the quality and quantity of ingredients dictate the power and volatility of the outcome.

## 1. The Core Mechanic: Templates vs. Infusion
Every item starts with a **Base Template** (e.g., *Broadsword Frame*, *Leather Vest Schema*).

- **Base Template:** Defines the core form and minimum required materials.
- **Resource Infusion:** Players can add additional resources into the template during the crafting process. Each infusion adds a "Stat Slot" or improves an existing value.

## 2. Stat Correlation
The type of resource added directly influences the item's property:
- **Iron/Steel:** Higher Physical Defense / Damage.
- **Gemstones:** Crystal Efficiency / Charge Retention.
- **Rare Animal Hides:** Weight Reduction / Elemental Resistance.
- **Portal Dust:** (Extreme Rare) High-tier traits like Weightlessness or Durability preservation.

## 3. The Risk: Volatility & Destruction
To prevent the creation of "God-Tier" items without consequence, adding resources increases **Volatility**.

- **Destruction Chance:** Every added resource beyond the base template increases the % chance that the item is destroyed upon completion.
- **The "Boom" Factor:** High Volatility failure doesn't just result in "no item"; it can consume all materials and potentially damage the crafting station.

## 4. Mitigation: Mastery & The "Rusty" Factor
A crafter's skill acts as a stabilizer for Volatility.

- **Rank Influence:** Each rank in a specific item skill (e.g., *Rank 50 Longswords*) reduces the baseline destruction chance.
- **The Rusty Penalty:** If a crafter has high Soul Debt or is in a "Rusty" state (see [03 Progression](file:///e:/FaldoranPrimeMMO/Documents/Design/03_Progression_and_Attributes.md)), their ability to stabilize Volatility drops significantly.
- **Master's Touch:** High-rank crafters can "Lock" certain stats, ensuring they don't roll poorly even if the item survives.

## 5. Economic Impact
- **No Two Items are the Same:** A "Steel Sword" crafted by a Master may have 5 infusion slots, while a novice's version has 1.
- **Material Sink:** The destruction chance creates a massive sink for high-tier resources, maintaining scarcity even as the server ages.
