# 04 Economy and Interdependence

The economy of Faldoran Prime is 100% player-driven, decentralized, and governed by the "Economy of Distance."

## Decentralized Financial Systems

### Guild-Created Currencies
- Guilds can mint their own **Currencies** to control local economies.
- Currencies can be backed by physical resources (Gold, Iron, Grain) held in guild vaults.
- Value is determined by trust and regional demand.

### Guild-Run Banks
- Banks facilitate currency conversion between different guild coins based on market exchange rates.
- Guilds can offer loans or interest to nomadic players or startups.

## The Economy of Distance ("Silk Road")
- **Regional Resources:** Iron might be in the North, Spices in the South. This forces trade routes.
- **Bandits and Guards:** Trade routes create gameplay opportunities for protection services and piracy.
- **Scarcity:** Resources migrate and deplete, preventing static "safe" farming.
- **Finite Deposits:** Major mineral sources do not respawn. This creates "Boom and Bust" economic cycles.

## The Death of a Hub (Economic Cycles)
As resources in a region are exhausted:
1.  **Inflation:** Local prices for basic goods skyrocket as they must be imported from further away.
2.  **Asset Liquidation:** Guilds may start deconstructing player-built towns for base materials (recycling) to move to a new shard.
3.  **Vassal Conflict:** Tenants might refuse to pay rent to a guild that can no longer provide local protection or resources.

## Logistics and Sustainability
- **Everything Breaks:** Items and buildings must be maintained or replaced, ensuring constant demand for crafters.
- **Fast Travel Taxes:** Portal-owning guilds collect 100% of fees, making portals vital economic hubs.
- **Starvation Sieges:** Cutting off trade to the farms supporting a portal is a valid tactical move to disable enemy infrastructure.
