# 08 NPC Factions and Standing

In an Earth-sized world, you aren't just a hero; you are a neighbor. Your actions dictate how the world reacts to your presence.

## 1. Standing Tiers
Reputation is tracked on a scale from **-10,000 (Arch-Nemesis)** to **+10,000 (Exalted)**.

- **Hostile (< -2,000):** NPCs attack on sight. Merchants refuse trade.
- **Neutral (-500 to 500):** Generic interactions. Standard pricing.
- **Ally (> 2,000):** Discounts, access to specialized NPC hirelings, and "Safe Haven" rights.

## 2. Regional vs. Global Standing
- **Global Factions:** Large entities (e.g., The Mage's Circle, The Merchant's Guild). Reputation carries across continents via Portals.
- **Regional Factions:** Local tribes or wildlife groups. Hunting local deer might anger a local "Protector" NPC, but a tribe 1,000km away won't care.

## 3. The "Civilization" Impact
- **Guild Sovereignty:** If a Guild is allied with a Faction, all members receive a portion of that standing.
- **Provocation:** Building a massive industrial town in a "Holy Forest" will slowly degrade standing with nature-aligned factions, potentially triggering "Raids" or "Natural Disasters."

## 4. NPC Hirelings (Group Support)
Interdependence extends to NPCs.
- **Portal Wardens:** Require food and wages. If Standing drops, they may "Strike" or sabotage the portal.
- **Guards:** Can be hired to patrol trade routes. Their effectiveness scales with the Guild's standing with the "Mercenary Company" faction.
