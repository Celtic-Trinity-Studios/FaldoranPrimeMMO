# 14 Titles and Professions

In Faldoran Prime, the absence of a rigid class system allows for a fluid and organic progression. Instead of choosing a class at the start, players earn **Titles** based on their skill ranks and combinations. These titles reflect a character's expertise and reputation within the world.

## 🏆 Title Mechanics

- **Automatic Recognition:** Titles are unlocked automatically when the required skill thresholds are met.
- **Dynamic Updates:** As a player shifts their focus (e.g., from swordplay to masonry), their active title may change to reflect their current status.
- **Tiered Titles:** Most titles have five ranks of prestige:
  - **Rank I:** Aspirant / Apprentice / Initiate
  - **Rank II:** Practitioner / Journeyman / Adept
  - **Rank III:** Specialist / Master / Expert
  - **Rank IV:** Paragon / Grandmaster / Artisan
  - **Rank V:** Legendary / Mythic / Arch-
- **Social & Mechanical Impact:** Titles can influence NPC reactions, unlock specialized quests, or provide subtle bonuses to the skill tree they represent.

These titles are earned by those who prioritize the art of war.

| Title | Required Skills | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vanguard** | Melee Prowess + Vitality + Heavy Melee | Front-line warriors who prioritize survival and heavy weaponry. |
| **Duelist** | Melee Prowess + Agility + Light Melee | Fast, precise fighters who rely on footwork and reflexes. |
| **Marksman** | Ranged Prowess + Archery + Dexterity | Experts in long-range engagement and precision. |
| **Skirmisher** | Skirmishing + Combat Reflexes + Athletics | Lightly armored scouts and guerilla fighters. |
| **Berserker** | Strength + Blunt Force + Courage | Power-focused combatants who use raw strength to crush foes. |
| **Gladiator** | Melee Prowess + Gauntlet Fighting + Athletics | Close-quarters brawlers and masters of unarmed/shielded combat. |
| **Assassin** | Death Blow + Skirmishing + Light Melee | Hidden killers focused on high-damage strikes to vital spots. |
| **Siege-Breaker** | Siege Weaponry + Strength + Damage Assessment | Specialists in dismantling fortifications and heavy constructs. |
| **Slayer** | Combat Sense + Any Lore (Beast/Abomin.) + Melee Prowess | Hunters dedicated to the eradication of specific monster types. |
| **Hoplite** | Melee Prowess + Stamina + Heavy Melee | Disciplined warriors who excel in formation and heavy armor. |
| **Bow-Knight** | Archery + Mount Training + Ranged Prowess | Masters of mobile, high-precision mounted archery. |
