# 01 Philosophy and Pillars

Faldoran Prime is the Ultimate MMO-Survival Hybrid, designed to maximize engagement while mitigating common genre pitfalls by shifting the survival focus from the individual to the group—**Civilization Building**.

## Core Pillars

### 1. The Living World (Dynamic Scarcity)
- **Migrating Resources:** Resources aren't static; herds move, forests regrow slowly, and seasons change availability.
- **Forced Migration:** Depleting local resources forces exploration, conflict, or trade with other regions.
- **Natural Disasters:** Server-wide events (blizzards, droughts) require community cooperation.

### 2. Interdependence Over Independence
- **Specialization:** No one can do everything. High-tier crafting requires materials from high-level "dungeons" (irradiated zones, caves), forcing crafters to hire mercenaries.
- **Survival Economy:** Food rots and gear breaks, creating constant demand for crafters and gatherers, preventing gold inflation.

### 3. Meaningful Building & Territory
- **Guild Towns:** Claiming territory grants distinct benefits (e.g., river control for irrigation).
- **Vulnerability Windows:** Bases are only attackable during specific times or declared wars to prevent offline griefing.

### 4. The Engagement Loop
1.  **Early Game (Survival):** Man vs. Nature.
2.  **Mid Game (Tribe):** Man vs. Tribe (Village building, specialized roles).
3.  **End Game (MMO):** Man vs. World (Nation-building, large-scale wars, raids).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Social Contract:** The game is not about the individual character; it is about the Civilization.
